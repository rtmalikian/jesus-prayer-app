# Jesus Prayer App — Virtual Orthodox Prayer Rope (Komboskini / Chotki)

**Live app:** https://rtmalikian.github.io/jesus-prayer-app/

A free, gentle web app for praying the **Jesus Prayer** — *"Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner"* — bead by bead on a **virtual Orthodox prayer rope**. Watch each wooden bead roll between finger and thumb the way a monk rolls his komboskini, say the prayer once per bead, and let the rhythm carry you into stillness.

Whether you are Orthodox, Catholic, Protestant, or simply seeking — this is a quiet corner of the internet, and you are welcome here.

## The Jesus Prayer

> *Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner.*

One of the oldest prayers of the Christian East. In a single breath it holds a whole confession of faith — Jesus is Lord, He is the Christ, He is the Son of God — joined to the tax collector's cry: *"God, be merciful to me, a sinner"* (Luke 18:13). Short enough to be prayed without ceasing; deep enough to pray for a lifetime.

## A sword and a shield

The Fathers speak of the Jesus Prayer as both weapon and protection — *"the shield of faith"* and *"the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God"* (Ephesians 6:16–17). As a sword, the invoked Name cuts through intrusive thoughts the moment they appear (St. Hesychios the Priest, *Philokalia*); as a shield, it becomes a covering carried through the day — *"The name of the LORD is a strong tower"* (Proverbs 18:10).

## Features

- **Virtual prayer rope** — 33, 50, or 100 wooden knots on an Orthodox-style rope (komboskini / chotki), drawn live on canvas
- **Monk-style bead-rolling animation** — each bead travels to the hand, is taken between thumb and index finger, rolls, and returns; the prayer text breathes with a soft golden pulse as you pray
- **Pray your way** — tap a bead, scroll, or press `Space` to advance; or switch on **auto-prayer** with three paces (Contemplative, Steady, Flowing)
- **Karaoke-style word highlighting** — in auto-prayer mode the words of the Jesus Prayer light up one by one at a natural speaking pace
- **Cross selector** — a discreet top-right picker with Armenian Apostolic (Oriental Orthodox, the default), Eastern Orthodox, Coptic, Greek Byzantine, and Ethiopian crosses; the cross pendant hangs at the bottom of the rope, as on a real komboskini, and always matches your choice
- **Prayer tally counter** — tracks beads prayed this sitting, rounds completed, and an all-time total saved on your device
- **Gentle wooden knock** — a soft synthesized bead sound (WebAudio, no audio files), mutable
- **Guide to the Jesus Prayer** — tap the ⓘ button for the prayer's biblical roots, its history from an **Oriental Orthodox perspective** (Desert Fathers, St. Isaac the Syrian, St. Gregory of Narek, the Philokalia), and how to begin
- **No account, no tracking** — everything runs in your browser; your tally never leaves your device
- **Mobile-friendly** — designed for the phone in your hand, respects `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Biblical roots of unceasing prayer

- **"Pray without ceasing."** — 1 Thessalonians 5:17
- **"Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!"** — Bartimaeus, Mark 10:47–48
- **"God, be merciful to me, a sinner."** — the tax collector, Luke 18:13
- **"Jesus, Master, have mercy on us."** — the ten lepers, Luke 17:13
- **The Holy Name** — "Whatever you ask in My name, that I will do" (John 14:13); "at the name of Jesus every knee should bow" (Philippians 2:10)

## The prayer rope tradition

Wool, for the flock of Christ; black, for mourning over sin; each knot woven with tiny crosses; the tassel, tradition says, for wiping the tears of repentance. Thirty-three knots for the years of Christ's earthly life — fifty, one hundred, three hundred for longer rules. Monks, and laypeople with them, have counted the Jesus Prayer on such ropes for over a thousand years.

## How to use

1. Open the [live app](https://rtmalikian.github.io/jesus-prayer-app/).
2. Choose your rope length (33, 50, or 100 beads).
3. Tap a bead (or scroll, or press `Space`) — each roll is one praying of the Jesus Prayer, said slowly, from the heart.
4. Or enable **auto-prayer** and let the rhythm carry you like breathing.
5. Tap **ⓘ** anytime to read about the prayer's roots and history.

No build step, no dependencies — just open `index.html` or serve the folder statically.
